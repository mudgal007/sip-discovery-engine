What the discovery engine analysed, where it was wrong, and how that was found

A record of the corpus behind the SIP continuation analysis, the quality gate applied to every label, and the three places the AI output was rejected or corrected. Compiled 9 November 2026.

The quality gate

A classifier asked to label 2,382 items will label 2,382 items, whether or not the text supports it. Every label therefore carries an evidence span, and code — not the model — decides whether that span is good enough to count.

An item is counted only if all three hold:

  • the evidence string is at least 8 characters;
  • the labelling was not marked low-confidence;
  • the quoted span literally appears in the source text, after normalising whitespace.

176 items the engine judged relevant were held back by this gate — 51% of everything it wanted to count. They are displayed in a separate table in the engine rather than silently dropped or silently counted.

Where the AI output was rejected or corrected

Correction 1 · systematic · caught by inspecting the distribution

The engine counted app complaints as SIP evidence

The first full pass over the corpus returned a distribution that looked wrong on its face: one trigger held three quarters of all counted items. Reading a random sample of those items showed why — the relevance rule had been underspecified, so any unhappy review qualified.

the worst broking app , many glitches whilentrading i won't recommend using it Labelled relevant · friction · counted
groww app F and O always struck, glitches,slow moments very bad, very worst app always lag Labelled relevant · friction · counted

Neither mentions a mutual fund. The prompt was rewritten to define relevance explicitly, name the exclusions — trading and F&O complaints, charts, brokerage, app crashes, login and KYC, support, wallet mechanics, contentless praise — restrict friction to friction inside the SIP journey, and state that a low relevance rate is the correct answer.

Correction 2 · single item · label overruled

An information gap was coded as a change of belief

Unaware about withdrawal rules. Unware that have to wait for a certain period just to with my very first month of investment. Screener free-text · engine said belief_collapse

The engine read this as the investor concluding the product was not working. It is not: nothing about their belief changed. They met a rule they did not know existed. The trigger is closer to friction, and the real signal is the information gap — exit_withdrawal_rules — which appears in 17.1% of counted items and is reported on its own rather than folded into a trigger.

Correction 3 · gate behaviour · accepted as correct

A false positive the gate caught before it was counted

absolutely scam high brokerage charge and oposite direction trade status today Market is down but now is going point its scam app dot trust Marked relevant · low confidence · held back, not counted

The relevance judgement was wrong — this is about brokerage and trading. The gate excluded it anyway, on confidence. This is recorded not as a defect but as the designed behaviour working: the model's judgement failed and the deterministic check caught it. It is also the reason the held-back count is reported rather than hidden; at 176 items it is a meaningful share of what the engine wanted to say.

What the gate does not check

The gate tests grounding and policy. It does not test whether an explanation is factually correct. During MVP testing a draft answer explained unit mathematics in a way that was subtly wrong — asserting that units bought at a lower NAV are therefore worth less than their purchase price — and passed every check, because nothing in it recommended a fund, predicted a return, or applied pressure. Numeric cases are the first thing the next iteration of the evaluation set should cover.

Correction 4 · single item · from the golden set

The engine read intent into a missing feature

After the recent update, the “Similar Funds in the Same Category” section has been removed. This feature was very useful when an existing fund was underperforming and I wanted to switch to a better-performing one. Play Store · engine said better_alternative · corrected to friction

The engine saw “switch to a better-performing one” and coded the person as leaving for an alternative. They are not leaving anything. They are describing a tool they can no longer use. The failure mode is general and worth naming: the engine weights surface vocabulary over what actually happened, which is the same error that produced Correction 1 at scale.

Reproducing any figure

Every number in the deck traces to data/labelled.json in the engine repository, which holds all 2,382 items with their full label set, evidence span, confidence and gate result. The superseded first run is kept alongside it as data/labelled_v1_loose_triage.json. The schema and the gate are in lib/schema.ts; the gate is the function passesGate, nine lines long.
